Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the FAQ page. Here you will find answers to commonly asked questions about using the Student Hub Portal.

How do I log in to the Student Hub Portal?

Use your registered student ID and password on the Login page.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click the Forgot Password link on the Login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.

What can I access from the dashboard?

The dashboard provides access to Profile, Attendance, Timetable and other student resources.

Why is my attendance not updated?

Attendance is updated periodically by the institution. If it is missing or incorrect, contact your department or portal support.

Where can I find study materials?

Study materials are available under the Study Materials section of your dashboard.

How do I view my semester results?

Go to the Results section and select the desired semester to view your marks.

The portal is not loading. What should I do?

Ensure you have an active internet connection. Refresh the page or try opening the portal in another browser.

Which browsers are supported?

The portal works best on the latest versions of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox and Safari.

How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact page for support email, phone number and working hours.
